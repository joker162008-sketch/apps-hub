How to practise

  1. Daily: listen with read-along, then switch to Repeat after me and say each sentence in the pause. Rate yourself 識講 (got it) or 再練 (again) — "again" sentences come back in later days.
  2. Dialogues: play the full script, then choose I play B to rehearse the candidate side out loud.
  3. Bank: search all sentences by Chinese, Jyutping, Mandarin or English.

Sample answers use a fictional candidate, 陳大文 (the Hong Kong placeholder name), with sample figures. Replace names, years, salaries and incidents with your own real experience before an interview.
